## 2024-10-02 - Contextual actions within mapped lists
**Learning:** Contextual actions within mapped lists must include descriptive, contextual `aria-label` attributes in French for screen reader accessibility (e.g., `aria-label={\`Retirer ${nom(member)}\`}`). `title` attributes alone are insufficient.
**Action:** Always ensure mapped list actions have context-specific `aria-label`s.
