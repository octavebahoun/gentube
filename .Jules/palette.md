## 2026-09-06 - Added proper ARIA labels and focus states to video player controls\n**Learning:** The video player controls in the storyboard editor (rewind, play/pause, fast-forward) lacked proper ARIA labels and focus states. This is a common pattern for custom media controls. Adding dynamic `aria-label` and `aria-pressed` to the Play/Pause button and focus rings to all buttons significantly improves screen reader and keyboard accessibility.\n**Action:** Always verify that custom icon-only media controls have both `aria-label` and keyboard focus states (`focus-visible`).
